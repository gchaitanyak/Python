About the certificate

What does it show?

Your name, the date it was issued, the modules you completed and a credential ID. Optional modules (13 and 14) are added when you finish them.

How do employers check it?

Every certificate has a public page at /certificate/<credential ID>/. Share the link, or they can search for the ID on this page.

Can I change my name later?

No — the name is fixed once the certificate is issued, so the record stays trustworthy. Check the spelling before you generate it.
